# Primal Peptides UK redesign concept

## Scope
- Replace the blank homepage with a concise, premium research-focused storefront.
- Add representative Shop, Product, Research Information, Contact and Checkout views.
- Use current public product and business information where it can be verified; clearly label prototype-only content.
- Keep the final structure straightforward enough to reproduce in WordPress and WooCommerce.

## Visual direction
- Use a restrained dark navy, blue and teal system with crisp white space, refined typography and compact product cards.
- Preserve the elephant and DNA idea through a temporary brand mark area until the final logo is supplied.
- Prioritise strong mobile layouts, clear navigation, visible calls to action and subtle motion only.

## Key interactions
- Repeat “For research purposes only” and “Not for human consumption” prominently across the homepage, shop, product and footer areas.
- Add a working cart prototype and an 18+ confirmation gate before checkout can continue.
- Include mobile navigation, product browsing, product-detail selection and clear contact links.

## Technical details
- Build separate TanStack routes for each major page with unique search and social metadata.
- Define all colours, type, shadows and motion as reusable semantic design tokens.
- Use local interface assets and icons; no unsupported claims, invented approvals, reviews, certifications or treatment guidance.
- Verify desktop and mobile layouts plus the purchase and age-confirmation flow in the live preview.
